Design

Measuring whether change lasts—and why.

The proposed quasi-experimental design combines matched intervention and comparison schools with baseline, post-program, and six-month follow-up measurement. A parallel process evaluation uses classroom observation, teacher logs, focus groups, and interviews to study implementation conditions.
