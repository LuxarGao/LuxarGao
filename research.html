ACWS · Seoul · 2026

Her New Shield

Digital Detours: Human–AI Companionship as Feminist Praxis and the Reimagining of Kinship in Post-Fertility China

This project treats AI-mediated intimacy not simply as social isolation, but as a deliberate negotiation of patriarchal domesticity. It studies how AI companions may meet affective needs while allowing women to protect bodily and reproductive sovereignty.

An explanatory sequential mixed-method design combines cross-platform discourse from Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and X with topic and sentiment analysis, followed by semi-structured interviews with 4B practitioners.

4S · Toronto · 2026

RUN to Be a Single Mom

Cross-Border ART for Migration among Chinese Single Women

Because single women in China are institutionally restricted from legal assisted reproductive technologies, cross-border ART can become both a medical choice and a migration strategy. The project analyzes more than 40,000 Rednote posts to investigate algorithms as informal reproductive infrastructures.

It asks how platform curation turns complex procedures into actionable migration guidebooks while amplifying elite independence narratives and obscuring financial and physical risks—creating a class-gated, consumerist promised land.
